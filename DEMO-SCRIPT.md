# ClearDoor demo script (about 90 seconds)

Record the screen at 1280x900 or on a phone. Speak slowly and clearly.
Captions or this transcript can go under the video.

## 0:00 - The barrier (15 seconds)

"This is a normal textbook paragraph about photosynthesis. Reading
level grade 16. Long sentences, hard words. For a student reading in a
second language, or with low vision, on a shared phone, this page is a
locked door."

Show: the Make it clear screen with the sample lesson loaded. Point at
the chips: grade 16.3, 25.6 words per sentence, 43 hard words.

## 0:15 - Make it clear (25 seconds)

"I press Make it clear. ClearDoor swaps formal words for everyday
words, and breaks long sentences into short ones. Grade 16 becomes
grade 10.8. Forty-three hard words become thirty-two. And I can see
every word it changed."

Show: click Make it clear. Point at the Before and After boxes. Open
"Words made plain".

## 0:40 - Save and read (30 seconds)

"I save it to my library. It lives on this device, no account, no
signal needed. In the reader I can make the text bigger, spread the
lines, switch to night or high contrast, tap a paragraph to focus on
it, or press Read aloud and listen."

Show: Save to library. Open Read. Move the size slider. Switch to
Night, then High contrast. Tap a paragraph. Press Read aloud for a
few seconds, then Stop.

## 1:10 - Offline library (15 seconds)

"My library keeps every lesson on the phone. When the signal drops,
the lessons are still here. I can also download them all as one text
file to print or share."

Show: My library screen with the lesson cards. Point at Download all.

## 1:25 - Close (10 seconds)

"ClearDoor opens one door: lesson text that is too hard to read. Plain
words, short sentences, big readable type, a voice, and an offline
library. Thank you."

Show: the ClearDoor header and the three tabs.
